Macarons

Macarons are dainty, meringue-based sandwich cookies with a crispy exterior and a soft, chewy interior, typically filled with buttercream, ganache, or jam. They are made with almond flour, egg whites, and sugar and are known for their smooth, shiny surfaces and ruffled edges, often called the "crown" or "foot".
