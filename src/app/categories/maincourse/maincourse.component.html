Fish as a food source is a vital source of high-quality protein and essential nutrients, particularly important in regions where livestock is scarce, and is a staple in many cultures, offering a unique combination of protein and vital nutrients that are not easily found in other food groups.

Indian Lunch

A typical Indian lunch is a flavorful and balanced meal often featuring rice or roti (flatbread), dal (lentils), a variety of vegetable curries, and sometimes meat or fish, accompanied by yogurt, pickles, and chutneys, with regional variations.

European Cuisine

European cuisine is a diverse and rich tapestry of flavors, reflecting the continent's varied geography, history, and cultures, encompassing everything from hearty stews and pastries to fresh seafood and bold cheeses.
